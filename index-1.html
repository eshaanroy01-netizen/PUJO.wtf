02 / The six-day spell

Pujo,
day by day.

Mahalaya starts the emotional countdown. Kolkata's main Puja days begin with Shashthi and run through Dashami.

01

Mahalaya

10 Oct · Devi Paksha begins. Wake before sunrise if nostalgia has a schedule.

02

Shashthi

16 Oct · Bodhon. Ma is here.

03

Saptami

18 Oct · Nabapatrika, bhog and pandal hopping.

04

Ashtami

19 Oct · Anjali. Sandhi Puja. Dhunuchi. The big one.

05

Navami

20 Oct · Bhog, arati and one last giant night.

06

Dashami

21 Oct · Sindoor. Mishti. Bisharjan. “Asche bochor abar hobe.”
