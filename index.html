03 / Bengal, condensed

Things that
make it Bengal.

No itinerary. No guidebook voice. Just the little things that somehow become the whole country when you miss it.

The language
“কোথায় পুজো দেখতে যাবি?”

Translation: Where are we going pandal hopping? Actual meaning: cancel everything else.

The bhog

Khichuri
fixes things.

Khichuri. Labra. Begun bhaja. Tomato chutney. Payesh. If you are far away, make one thing from this list and your kitchen gets a little louder.

88.8 FM
Bengal Radio

Adda FM

You are listening from somewhere far away. The station is still yours.
